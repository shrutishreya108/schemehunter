WELFARE PROGRAMMES INFORMATION

Under Udyogini scheme, women can avail a loan from Rs.1.00 Lakhs to Rs.3.00 Lakhs from banks.

Eligibility criteria and Required Documents:-

  • Age limit 18 to 55 Years
  • Income and Caste Certificate (Annual Income limit for General / Special Category below Rs.1.50 lakhs, SC / ST Category below Rs.2.00 lakhs)
