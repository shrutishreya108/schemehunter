(xii) “Micro and Small Enterprises” As per the MSMED Act, 2006 an
“enterprise” means an industrial undertaking or a business concern or
any other establishment, by whatever name called, engaged in the

industry specified in the First Schedule to the Industries (Development
and Regulation) Act, 1951 or engaged in providing or rendering of any
service or services; and “Micro and Small Enterprises” are defined in
7.1.a.i) and ii) & in 7.1.b.i) and ii) of the said Act and amended from
time to time.
(xiii) “Tenure of guarantee cover” means the maximum period of
guarantee cover from Guarantee sanction date which shall run through
the agreed tenure of the term credit and for a period of 5 years or block
of a 5 years from Guarantee start date where working capital facilities
alone are extended or loan termination date, whichever is earlier or
such period as may be specified by the Trust. The guarantee expiry
date for all term loan accounts shall be agreed tenure + four (4)
months.
(xiv) “Trust” means the Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small
Enterprises set up by Government of India and SIDBI with the purpose
of guaranteeing credit facility(ies), extended by the lending
institution(s) to the eligible borrowers.

Limited Company; or (vi) the owner of the collateral security in the case
