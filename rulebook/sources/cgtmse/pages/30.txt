Annexure IV
Under Hybrid Security Model : scenarios for fee and claim calculation
Scenario 1
Guarantee Limit
Term Loan
Loan Sanctioned: ₹2 Crore
Collateral value: ₹1 Crore
Maximum guarantee Coverage (₹2 crore less ₹1 Crore) = ₹1 Crore
Fee Calculation
Outstanding as on December 31 for Term Loan is ₹1.8 Crore
Amount on which fee will be calculated:
₹1.8 crore (outstanding) – ₹1 crore (collateral Value) = ₹0.8 crore
The fee will be calculated on ₹0.8 Crore
Claim Limit
Maximum Limit would be on which fee is paid = ₹0.8 Crore
