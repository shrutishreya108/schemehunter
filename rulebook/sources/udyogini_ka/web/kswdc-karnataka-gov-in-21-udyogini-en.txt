WELFARE PROGRAMMES INFORMATION
This scheme intends to uplift women belonging to economically weaker section and empower them with financially assistance women who are willing to become self-employed in business and service sector are provided with subsidy through Karnataka State Women Development Corporation for the loan provide by the bank to take up financial activities and make them economically independent.
Under Udyogini scheme, women can avail a loan from Rs.1.00 Lakhs to Rs.3.00 Lakhs from banks for which subsidy of 30% is provided to General and Special category women and Subsidy of 50% is provided to SC / ST category women.
Eligibility criteria and Required Documents:-
Permanent resident of Karnataka
Age limit 18 to 55 Years
Aadhar card
Residential certificate / Voter ID / Ration Card
Project Report pertaining to activity
Aadhar seeding is the beneficiaries bank account is mandatory
Income and Caste Certificate provided by concerned Tahsildars (Annual Income limit for General / Special Category below Rs.1.50 lakhs , SC / ST Category below Rs.2.00 lakhs)
