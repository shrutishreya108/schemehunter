In supersession of this Ministry’s O.M. No. PMEGP/Policy/09/2021 dated 13,05,2022
issuing the revised guidelines of Prime Minister’s Employment Generation Programme for
continuation of the scheme over the 15th Finance Commission cycle for five years from
2021-22 to 2025-26, the scheme guidelines has been further revised having
insertion/modification of the following clauses: -
a “All new units setup under PMEGP will be mandatorily registered under Udyam
Portal before Physical Verification of the Unit and the adjustment of the Margin
Money in the PMEGP beneficiary loan account.” (Other eligibility conditions for
PMEGP (New Units) – (Clause 4.1 )
b “Registration with the KVIC/KVIBs/State DICs under the scheme is voluntary.
Besides, all the PMEGP units will have to be mandatorily registered undel
Udyam Portal before adjustment of Margin Money in beneficiary loan account.
No registration fee will be charged from the berleficiaries and the funds
available under Backward and Forward Linkage wili be utilized to meet
expenses on documentation cost, etc.” (Clause 26)
